## 1. Why the pulse strip lives in the renderer and the SDK does not

They sit on opposite sides of one trust boundary.

- The SmartSpectra SDK is native Node: it needs the camera and OS capture, and it has no browser build. The renderer is Chromium with `sandbox: true`, `contextIsolation: true`, `nodeIntegration: false` and a CSP (`src/main/index.ts:117-122`). Hosting the SDK there would mean dismantling the sandbox that makes pasted PGNs and AI text safe.
- The renderer is a painter. It has no `fs`, no keys, no engine. It receives `PulseSample[]` — `{tUs, bpm, confidence, stable}` — over `cp:sample:batch` and renders it (`src/renderer/App.tsx:23-30`, `src/preload/index.ts:60-90`).
- Only numbers cross IPC; frames never do. Main validates with `sampleBatchSchema` (`src/main/index.ts:24-30`), preload re-checks the shape, and the renderer validates again before appending. Three checks, one boundary, deliberately.
- Domain math stays out of the renderer. Baseline and coverage are computed in `src/main/session.ts`; the strip imports only the pure gap rule and constants from `src/shared/sensing.ts` so drawing and coverage cannot disagree about what a hole is.

## 2. What coverage counts

Coverage is wall-clock time covered by usable signal — not sample count, not mean confidence (`coverageFromSamples`, `src/main/session.ts:61-84`).

- Denominator: `stopUs − startUs`, the real session span.
- A sample counts only inside the window and only if `stable === true && confidence >= 50` (`CONFIDENCE_FLOOR`).
- Each usable sample credits time until the next sample, capped at one second (`SAMPLE_HOLD_US`). The last usable sample credits to `stopUs`, also capped at 1 s. Gaps beyond the cap earn nothing.
- Unusable samples credit zero while remaining in the raw NDJSON trace.
- `pct = usableUs / totalUs × 100`, clamped at 100; an empty session is 0%.

Consequence: a 30 s face-loss hole costs 29 s of coverage (the sample before it still covers 1 s), and dense high-confidence sampling cannot paper over a gap.

## 3. Why a dropped sample is not a zero

"No measurement" and "a pulse of zero" are different claims, and only one is true.

- Zero bpm is physiologically meaningless here; encoding a dropout as 0 would fabricate a measurement and drag the baseline median down.
- Honest treatment, already implemented: drop from aggregation, keep on the raw trace, let coverage fall. Low-confidence samples never enter `baselineFromSamples` and earn no coverage credit; the dropout stays visible in the file.
- The strip enforces the same rule: `traceSegments` breaks the polyline at gaps over 1 s and draws isolated samples as dots (`src/shared/sensing.ts:13-30`, `src/renderer/SensingStrip.tsx:63-74`). A gap is drawn as a gap, never interpolated.
- When too much is unknown, the app refuses (`Refusal{reason:'low_coverage'}` with measured value, threshold, remedy) rather than averaging over the unknown.
